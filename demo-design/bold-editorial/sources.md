# Sources — Bold Editorial

All six were originally saved under `maximalism/`. They turned out to share one pattern that no
existing guide covered — a single loud colour, type at poster scale, cut-out imagery on cream,
and a visible grid — which is why `design-guide/bold-editorial.md` exists.

Source URLs weren't recorded when these were saved; the original filename is the provenance
trace. `pinterest_website_design_inspiration.html` may hold the matching pins.

**What these establish:** the loud colour has to appear as a large field, not just on buttons;
imagery is always cut out, duotone or dithered; and the grid stays visible underneath through
rules, indices and edge labels.
